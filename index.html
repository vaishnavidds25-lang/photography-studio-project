7. Business Improvement Suggestions

  1. Optimize Google Business Profile: Actively collect 5-star reviews with photo feedback from happy clients to dominate local Google search.
  2. WhatsApp Automation Chatbot: Add a direct WhatsApp chat button on the website to send instant price quotes and PDF brochures.
  3. Behind-The-Scenes (BTS) Short Videos: Leverage Instagram Reels showing shoot fun and transparent working methods to build trust.
  4. Referral Discount Incentive: Offer existing clients ₹1,000 credit or a free canvas print for every friend they refer who books a shoot.
  5. Seasonal & Festival Bundles: Create tailored packages for peak wedding seasons, Valentine's Day, and Diwali commercial cataloging.
  6. Track Campaign Performance Metrics: Monitor Instagram Reel reach, Google Ad click-through rates (CTR), monthly website inquiries, and booking conversions.
